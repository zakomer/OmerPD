Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to provide water or power?

Access to an outdoor water spigot is ideal. If that's not available at your location, let us know when booking and we'll work out an alternative.

How long does a detail take?

Most exterior services take 45–90 minutes. Full packages and the Full Reset typically run 2–4 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Do you travel outside of Kent?

Yes, we regularly serve the surrounding area. Enter your address at booking and we'll confirm availability and any travel fee.

What if my car needs more than a standard clean?

Heavier soiling, pet hair, or biohazard situations are priced separately and confirmed on-site before we start — no surprise charges after the fact.

How do I pay?

Payment is collected on completion. We'll confirm accepted methods when we get in touch to schedule your appointment.
